## General

**Is Furrcircle free?**
Yes - Furrcircle is free to download and use for pet parents.

**Where is Furrcircle available?**
It's built for pet parents in India and is available as a mobile app. Some features depend on your
city and the app version.

**Can I add more than one pet?**
Absolutely. Each pet gets their own profile, gallery and
[health passport](/furrcircle/guide/features/pet-health-passport).

## Social & community

**What are Circles?**
Local, interest-based communities of pet parents. See
[Circles](/furrcircle/guide/features/circles).

**What's the difference between Stories and Reels?**
Stories are 24-hour moments that disappear; Reels are full-screen, swipeable pet videos. See
[Stories & Reels](/furrcircle/guide/features/stories-and-reels).

## Match & adoption

**What is Match?**
A swipe-to-connect feature with three modes - Playdate, Adoption and Breed. See
[Match](/furrcircle/guide/features/match).

**How does adoption work?**
Browse adoptable pets, submit a request, and chat with the current carer to arrange a responsible
handover. See [Apply to adopt](/furrcircle/guide/how-to/apply-to-adopt).

## Health

**Does the health passport replace my vet?**
No. It helps you organise and remember records and reminders, but always consult your vet for
medical advice. See [Pet health passport](/furrcircle/guide/features/pet-health-passport).

**Can I book a vet in the app?**
Yes - find nearby vets and book directly through
[Discover](/furrcircle/guide/features/discover).

## Lost pets

**My pet is missing - what do I do?**
Open [Lost & Found](/furrcircle/guide/features/lost-and-found) and report them right away so the
local community is alerted. See [Report a lost pet](/furrcircle/guide/how-to/report-a-lost-pet).

:::tip Staying safe
Read [Safety & Privacy](/furrcircle/guide/safety-and-privacy) before meeting people for playdates
or adoptions.
:::
