**Circles** are Furrcircle's communities - groups of pet parents organised by **city** and
**interest**. They're where the app gets local and genuinely useful.

## What circles are

- **City-based** - connect with pet parents in your area.
- **Interest-based** - Dogs, Cats, Rescue, Health, Training, and custom topics.
- A place to find playdates, vet recommendations, and meetups near you.

## Community Q&A

Inside a circle you can ask and answer questions:

- **Ask** the community anything - from "best vet in Gachibowli?" to training tips.
- **Answer** others and share what you know.
- Helpful answers rise with **upvotes**, and the best can be marked as **accepted**.

## How to take part

1. Open the **Circles** tab and **join** circles that fit.
2. **Post** and share within the circle.
3. **Ask** a question, or **answer** someone else's.
4. Use circle recommendations to find vets, places and events.

## Search across Furrcircle

Looking for something specific? Search spans people, pets, posts, circles, questions and
hashtags, so you can always find your way to the right community.

:::tip Be the helpful neighbour
Circles thrive on generosity. Answer a couple of questions and you'll quickly become a familiar,
trusted face in your local pet community.
:::
