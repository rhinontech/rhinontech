**Discover** is your local pet map. Furrcircle uses your city to surface the vets, pets and
places around you - so help and connection are always close by.

## What you can find

- **Nearby vets** - browse, search and sort, view profiles and read reviews.
- **Book appointments** - schedule a vet visit directly from the app.
- **Nearby pets** - pets available for adoption, foster or breeding near you.
- **Local places** - spots for your pet's needs in your area.

## How it works

1. Open **Discover** - it auto-detects your **city**.
2. Browse **vets**, **pets** or **places**.
3. Open a **vet profile** to see details and reviews.
4. **Book** an appointment in a few taps.

## Reviews you can trust

Vet profiles carry reviews from real pet parents, so you can choose with confidence - and leave
your own after a visit to help the community.

:::tip Keep your city current
If you move or travel, update your location so Discover shows the right vets, pets and places
around you.
:::
