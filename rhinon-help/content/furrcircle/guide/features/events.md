**Events** help you take the community offline. Discover pet meetups and gatherings near you, and
let people know you're coming.

## What it does

- **Discover local events** - meetups, gatherings and pet-friendly happenings near you.
- **RSVP** - let organisers and others know you'll be there.
- Bring your **circle** together in real life.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Events** section.
2. Browse what's happening near you.
3. **RSVP** to the ones you'd like to attend.
4. Show up and meet the community - and their pets.

:::tip Meet your feed in real life
Events are the best way to turn online friends into real ones. If your circle is planning a
meetup, RSVP and bring your pet along.
:::
