The **Feed** is your home screen on Furrcircle - a stream of posts from the pets and parents you
follow, mixed with fresh suggestions so you always have something delightful to scroll.

## What you'll see

- **Posts from people you follow** - photos, videos and updates from pets you love.
- **Suggested for you** - new pets, parents and circles you might enjoy.
- **A pet-first vibe** - it's all pets, all the time. No noise.

## What you can do

- **Like, comment and share** posts.
- **Save** posts you want to come back to.
- **Follow** pets and parents to fill your feed with more of what you love.
- Tap any profile to explore a pet's full gallery and story.

## Why it's different

Generic social apps weren't built for pets. The Furrcircle feed is - so the people, content and
suggestions are all about the thing you actually came for.

:::tip Follow generously
The more pets and parents you follow, the richer your feed becomes. Start with your
[city circle](/furrcircle/guide/getting-started/join-your-city-circle) members.
:::
