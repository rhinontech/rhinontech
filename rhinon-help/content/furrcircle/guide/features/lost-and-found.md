Few things are scarier than a missing pet. **Lost & Found** turns Furrcircle's local community
into a search party when it matters most.

## What it does

- **Report a lost pet** - alert the community near you with photos and details.
- **Report a found or spotted pet** - help reunite a pet you've seen with their family.
- **Live status tracking** - follow updates as the search progresses.
- **Community help** - nearby pet parents can keep an eye out and share leads.

## If your pet goes missing

1. Open **Lost & Found** and choose **Report lost**.
2. Add a clear **photo**, **description** and **last-seen location**.
3. Post it - nearby members are alerted.
4. **Update the status** as you get information, and mark it resolved when they're home.

## If you spot a pet

1. Choose **Report found / spotted**.
2. Add a photo and where you saw them.
3. Post it so their family can find the alert.

:::info Speed and reach matter
The first hours are the most important. A clear photo and an accurate last-seen location give the
community the best chance to help.
:::
