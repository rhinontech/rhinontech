**Match** is Furrcircle's swipe-to-connect experience for pets. Depending on what you're looking
for, it has three modes.

## Playdate mode

Find **nearby, compatible pets** for weekend meetups. Swipe, match, and when it's mutual you can
chat and plan a playdate.

## Adoption mode

Discover **pets looking for a home**. You can:

- Browse adoptable pets near you.
- **Submit an adoption request** for one you connect with.
- If you're rehoming, **list a pet** and manage the requests you receive.

## Breed mode

An **opt-in** mode for responsible, compatible breeding matches between pets whose parents choose
to take part.

## How matching works

1. Open **Match** and choose a mode: Playdate, Adoption or Breed.
2. **Swipe** through nearby pets.
3. On a **mutual match**, start a **chat** instantly.
4. Manage incoming requests from your **requests inbox** (accept or decline).

:::info Adoption is a real commitment
Adoption requests connect you with the current carer to arrange a responsible handover. Take the
time to chat, ask questions, and make sure it's the right fit for everyone - especially the pet.
:::
