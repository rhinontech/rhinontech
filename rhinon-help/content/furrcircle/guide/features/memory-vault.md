The **Memory Vault** is where your pet's best moments are kept and celebrated - a beautiful,
year-by-year collection of their life.

## What it is

- A **year-by-year photo vault** for each pet (their "Pet Aura").
- A place to revisit how they've grown, season by season.
- **Birthday and gotcha-day** tracking and celebrations.

## Why pet parents love it

Pets grow up fast. The Memory Vault makes sure the moments don't get lost in your camera roll - it
organises them by year so you can look back and celebrate together.

## How to use it

1. Keep [posting](/furrcircle/guide/features/posts-and-profiles) your pet's moments.
2. Open your pet's **Memory Vault** to see them organised by year.
3. Celebrate **birthdays and gotcha days** when they come around.

:::tip Mark the milestones
Add your pet's birthday or gotcha day to their profile so Furrcircle can remind you and help you
celebrate each year.
:::
