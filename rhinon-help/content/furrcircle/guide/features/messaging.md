**Messaging** keeps the conversation going. Whenever you connect with another pet parent on
Furrcircle, you can chat directly to plan, coordinate or simply share.

## What you can do

- **Direct messages** with other pet parents.
- **Rich sharing** - send photos and moments in chat.
- Pick up instantly after a **[Match](/furrcircle/guide/features/match)** to plan a playdate or
  discuss an adoption.

## When you'll use it

- Arranging a **playdate** after a mutual match.
- Coordinating an **adoption** with a pet's current carer.
- Following up with someone you met in a **circle**.

## How to start a chat

1. Open a pet parent's profile, or a match/request.
2. Tap **Message**.
3. Start chatting - and share photos as you go.

:::tip Keep first chats friendly and clear
Especially for playdates and adoptions, a warm, specific first message ("Would Saturday morning at
the park suit you?") gets things moving.
:::
