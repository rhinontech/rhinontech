The **Pet Health Passport** is one of Furrcircle's most important features. It replaces the drawer
full of paper cards with a complete, always-available digital record of your pet's health.

## What it keeps

- **Vaccination history** - what was given and when.
- **Medications** - current and past.
- **Allergies** - so they're never forgotten in an emergency.
- **Vitals** - temperature, heart rate, weight, blood pressure.
- **Insurance and microchip** details.

## Care reminders

The passport doesn't just store - it **reminds**:

- Upcoming **vaccinations** and **boosters**.
- **Deworming** and routine **checkups**.
- So nothing important slips past a busy schedule.

## Log things in seconds

1. Open your pet's **health** section.
2. Add a record - a **vaccination**, a **medication**, or a **vital** like weight or temperature.
3. Save - it's now part of their passport, and reminders update automatically.

## Why it matters

When you're at the vet, travelling, or facing an emergency, having your pet's full history in your
pocket makes every decision faster and safer.

:::warning Not a substitute for veterinary care
The health passport helps you organise and remember - it doesn't replace professional advice.
Always consult your vet for diagnosis and treatment.
:::
