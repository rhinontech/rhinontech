Profiles and posts are how your pet's story lives on Furrcircle.

## Profiles

Both **you** and each of your **pets** get a profile:

- **Pet profiles** - photo gallery, bio, breed, birthday, followers and following.
- **Your profile** - ties your pets together and represents you in the community.
- Followers and following counts, so you can see your pet's growing circle of friends.

## Posts

A post can include:

- **Photos and videos**
- A **caption** to tell the story
- A **location** to remember where it happened

Friends can **like**, **comment**, **share** and **save** your posts.

## How to post

1. Tap **Create** (the + button) and choose **Post**.
2. Add your photos or video.
3. Write a caption and tag a location.
4. Share it to your followers and feed.

:::tip Keep the gallery lovely
Your pet's profile gallery is their highlight reel. Post the moments you'll want to look back on -
the [Memory Vault](/furrcircle/guide/features/memory-vault) keeps the best of them by year.
:::
