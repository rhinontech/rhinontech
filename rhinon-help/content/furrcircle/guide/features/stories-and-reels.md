Furrcircle gives your pet two fun ways to shine beyond regular posts: **Stories** and **Reels**.

## Stories

- **24-hour moments** - share a quick photo or video that disappears after a day.
- **Location-aware** - perfect for "we're at the park!" moments.
- Great for the small, everyday bits that don't need to live on your profile forever.

## Reels

- **Full-screen, swipeable videos** - the fun, bingeable side of Furrcircle.
- Swipe through a never-ending stream of adorable pet clips.
- A great place to discover new pets and parents to follow.

## How to share

1. Tap **Create** (the + button).
2. Choose **Story** or **Reel**.
3. Add your photo or video, and any caption or location.
4. Share - and watch the love roll in.

:::tip Reels for reach
Reels are the easiest way for new people to find your pet. A short, charming clip goes a long way.
:::
