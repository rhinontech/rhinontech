The **Today** hub is your daily check-in. It gathers what matters for your pets right now, so you
start each day knowing what's coming up.

## What you'll see

- **Today's reminders** - vaccines, medications and checkups due.
- **Schedules** - what's planned for your pets.
- **Upcoming birthdays** - yours and your friends' pets.
- **Playdate suggestions** - nearby pets to meet.

## Why it's handy

Instead of digging through different sections, the Today hub brings care, calendar and community
into a single, scannable view - perfect for a quick morning glance.

:::tip Make it a habit
A ten-second look at Today each morning means you never miss a booster, a checkup, or a friend's
pet's birthday.
:::
