Your **pet's profile** is the centre of Furrcircle. It's where their photos live, where their
health records are kept, and how they connect with other pets and parents. You can add more than
one pet.

## Set up the profile

1. Tap **Add pet**.
2. Add their **name**, **photo**, **species and breed**, and **birthday** (or gotcha day).
3. Add a short **bio** - let their personality shine.
4. Save - your pet now has a home on Furrcircle.

## Why the details matter

- **Birthday / gotcha day** powers celebrations and reminders.
- **Breed and age** help with relevant health reminders and better matches.
- **Photos** make their profile and your posts come alive.

## More than one pet?

Add each of them. Every pet gets their own profile, gallery and health passport, and you manage
them all from your account.

:::tip Start the health passport early
While you're here, add what you know - last vaccination, any allergies or medications. It only
takes a minute and means reminders work from day one. See the
[Pet health passport](/furrcircle/guide/features/pet-health-passport).
:::
