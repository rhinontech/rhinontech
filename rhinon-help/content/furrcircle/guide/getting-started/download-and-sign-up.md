Furrcircle is a mobile app for pet parents. Getting started takes just a couple of minutes.

## Download the app

1. Open the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android).
2. Search for **Furrcircle**.
3. Install and open the app.

## Create your account

1. Sign up with your **phone number** or **email**.
2. Verify your details.
3. Set up your **personal profile** - your name, photo and city.

Furrcircle uses your city to show you nearby circles, vets, events and pets, so it helps to set
it accurately.

:::tip Allow notifications
Turn on notifications so you don't miss care reminders, playdate requests, messages, and lost-pet
alerts in your area.
:::

## What's next

Now bring your pet into the app - [create a pet profile](/furrcircle/guide/getting-started/create-a-pet-profile).
