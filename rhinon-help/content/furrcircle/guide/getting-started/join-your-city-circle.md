**Circles** are Furrcircle's local communities - groups of nearby pet parents organised by city
and interest. Joining one is the fastest way to make the app feel like home.

## Find a circle

1. Open the **Circles** tab.
2. Browse circles near you - for example *Hyderabad Indie Dogs & Paws* or
   *Gachibowli Pet Parents Club*.
3. Filter by interest - Dogs, Cats, Rescue, Health, Training and more.
4. Tap **Join** on the ones that fit.

## What you can do in a circle

- Share posts and photos with people nearby.
- Ask and answer questions in the community **Q&A**.
- Find playdates, vet recommendations and local meetups.

## Why join

A good circle is where you get trusted, local advice - the vet everyone recommends, the park
that's dog-friendly, the people whose pets your pet will actually meet.

:::tip Join more than one
You might join a city circle *and* an interest circle (like a breed or rescue group). Mix and
match to get the right mix of local and topical.
:::
