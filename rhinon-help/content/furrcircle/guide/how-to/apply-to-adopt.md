Furrcircle helps adoptable pets find loving homes through
[Match](/furrcircle/guide/features/match) and [Discover](/furrcircle/guide/features/discover).

## Steps

1. Open **Match** and switch to **Adoption** mode (or browse adoptable pets in **Discover**).
2. **Swipe through** nearby pets looking for homes.
3. When one connects with you, **submit an adoption request**.
4. The current carer reviews requests and can **accept** to start a conversation.
5. **Chat** to ask questions and arrange a responsible handover.

## Be ready to adopt well

- Be honest about your home, time and experience.
- Ask about the pet's **health**, **temperament** and **routine**.
- Plan the handover and the first few days carefully.

:::info Adoption is a lifelong commitment
Take your time and make sure it's the right match for your life *and* the pet's needs. A good fit
is worth the extra conversations.
:::
