Furrcircle lets you find and book a vet without leaving the app, using
[Discover](/furrcircle/guide/features/discover).

## Steps

1. Open **Discover** and go to **Vets**.
2. Browse or **search** vets near you; sort to find the right fit.
3. Open a **vet profile** to see details and **reviews**.
4. Choose **Book appointment** and pick a time.
5. Confirm - you'll have the booking details in the app.

## After booking

- Keep the visit notes in your pet's
  [health passport](/furrcircle/guide/features/pet-health-passport).
- After the appointment, **leave a review** to help other pet parents.

:::tip Read the reviews
Reviews from real pet parents are the best way to choose a vet you'll trust. Skim a few before you
book.
:::
