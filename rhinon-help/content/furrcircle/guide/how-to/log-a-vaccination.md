Keeping vaccinations up to date in the [health passport](/furrcircle/guide/features/pet-health-passport)
means you'll always have the record on hand - and reminders for what's next.

## Steps

1. Open your **pet's profile** and go to the **Health** section.
2. Choose **Add vaccination**.
3. Enter the **vaccine name** and the **date** it was given.
4. Add any notes (vet, batch, next-due date) if you have them.
5. **Save**.

## After saving

- The vaccination appears in your pet's **history**.
- Reminders for **boosters** or the next dose update automatically.
- The record is always available - handy at the vet or while travelling.

:::tip Catch up in one sitting
If you're just starting, add your pet's recent vaccinations from their existing card so the
passport is accurate from day one.
:::
