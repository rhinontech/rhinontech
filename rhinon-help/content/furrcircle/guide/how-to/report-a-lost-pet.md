If your pet goes missing, acting fast gives the community the best chance to help. Here's how to
raise the alarm with [Lost & Found](/furrcircle/guide/features/lost-and-found).

## Steps

1. Open **Lost & Found** and choose **Report lost**.
2. Add a **clear, recent photo** of your pet.
3. Enter their **description** and **last-seen location and time**.
4. Add your **contact preference** so people can reach you.
5. **Post** - nearby Furrcircle members are alerted.

## Keep it moving

- **Update the status** as you receive tips or sightings.
- Share the alert with your [circles](/furrcircle/guide/features/circles) for extra reach.
- Mark it **resolved** when your pet is safely home.

:::warning The first hours count most
Post as soon as you realise they're missing. A clear photo and an accurate last-seen spot make all
the difference.
:::
