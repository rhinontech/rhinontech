Playdates are one of the most joyful parts of Furrcircle. Here's how to find one with
[Match](/furrcircle/guide/features/match).

## Steps

1. Open **Match** and choose **Playdate** mode.
2. **Swipe** through nearby, compatible pets.
3. On a **mutual match**, a [chat](/furrcircle/guide/features/messaging) opens.
4. **Plan the meetup** - suggest a place, day and time.
5. Meet up and let the pets make friends.

## Make the first meet a good one

- Pick a **neutral, safe** spot like a park.
- Keep the first meeting **short** and watch how they get along.
- Bring water and treats.

:::tip Turn suggestions into plans
Check the [Today hub](/furrcircle/guide/features/today-hub) for playdate suggestions - it's an easy
way to find pets to match with near you.
:::
