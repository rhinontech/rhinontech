Furrcircle is **India's all-in-one app for pet parents**. It brings together the things pet
parents juggle across a dozen apps - sharing photos, tracking vaccinations, finding a vet,
arranging playdates, adopting, and getting help when a pet goes missing - into **one app built
just for pets**.

## Why Furrcircle exists

Pet parents deserve better than generic social feeds and a drawer full of paper vaccination
cards. Furrcircle is built around three big ideas:

- **A pet-first social network** - a feed, stories and reels made for pets and the people who
  love them.
- **A digital health passport** - every vaccination, medication and vital safely in one place.
- **A local community** - circles, playdates, vet discovery and adoption near you.

## Who it's for

- **Everyday pet parents** who want a dedicated space for their pet's life.
- **Health-conscious owners** who want organised, always-available medical records.
- **Neighbours and local communities** looking for playdates, vets and meetups.
- **Rescuers, fosters and adopters** matching pets with loving homes.

## What you can do

- Build a profile for **you and your pets**, and share their moments.
- Join **Circles** - local, interest-based communities.
- Use **Match** to find playdates, adoptable pets, or responsible breeding matches.
- **Discover** nearby vets, places and pets, and book appointments.
- Keep a full **health passport** with reminders for vaccines and checkups.
- Report and resolve **lost & found** pets with community help.
- Celebrate milestones with a year-by-year **memory vault**.

## Free to use

Furrcircle is free to download and use for pet parents. Get it on your phone, create your pet's
profile, and you're in.
