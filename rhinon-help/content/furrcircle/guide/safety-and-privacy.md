Furrcircle is about real connections between pet parents - which means a little common-sense
safety goes a long way. Here are habits worth keeping.

## Meeting people

- Arrange first meetings in **public, neutral places** (a park is ideal).
- Tell a friend where you're going and when.
- Trust your instincts - if something feels off, you don't have to proceed.

## Playdates

- Keep the **first meeting short** and watch how the pets interact.
- Choose a **safe, enclosed or quiet** space.
- Don't force interactions - let the pets set the pace.

## Adoptions

- Ask plenty of **questions** about the pet's health, history and temperament.
- Plan a **responsible handover** and a settling-in period.
- Be honest about your own home and lifestyle so it's the right fit both ways.

## Your information

- Share contact details only when **you're comfortable**.
- Keep early conversations **inside the app** until you trust the other person.
- Report anything that seems unsafe or inappropriate.

:::warning In an emergency
For a sick or injured pet, contact a **vet** immediately - see
[Discover](/furrcircle/guide/features/discover) to find one near you. Furrcircle helps you organise
and connect, but it isn't a substitute for professional or emergency care.
:::
