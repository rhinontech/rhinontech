# Invite and manage your team

Workspace owners and admins can invite teammates and control what each member can do.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Select **Invite member** and enter their email.
3. Choose a role and send the invite.

Invited people receive an email and appear as **Pending** until they accept.

## Roles at a glance

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing and deletion |
| Admin | Manage members, tokens and settings |
| Member | Build and view, no billing access |
| Viewer | Read‑only access |

:::tip
Grant the lowest role that still lets someone do their job — you can always upgrade later.
:::

## Remove access

Open **Settings → Members**, select the member, and choose **Remove**. Their tokens are
revoked immediately.
