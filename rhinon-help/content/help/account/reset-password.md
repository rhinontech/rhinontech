# Reset your password

If you've forgotten your password or think your account may be compromised, you can reset
it in a couple of minutes.

## Steps

1. Go to the sign‑in page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Open the reset email and click **Choose a new password** (the link expires in 30 minutes).
4. Set a new password and sign in.

:::note
If your workspace uses single sign‑on, password reset is handled by your identity provider
instead. Contact your workspace admin.
:::

## Didn't get the email?

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Confirm you entered the email tied to your account.
- Wait a minute and request the link again.

Still stuck? [Contact support](/help) and we'll help you regain access.
