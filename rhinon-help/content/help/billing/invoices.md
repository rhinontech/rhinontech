# Find your invoices

Every payment generates an invoice you can download for your records.

## Download an invoice

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
2. Find the period you need.
3. Select **Download PDF**.

## Update billing details

Keep your company name, address and tax ID current so they appear correctly on invoices.
Edit them under **Settings → Billing → Billing details** — changes apply to future
invoices.

:::note
Need an invoice reissued with corrected details? [Contact support](/help) with the invoice
number.
:::
