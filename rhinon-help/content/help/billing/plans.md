# Plans and upgrades

Rhinon offers three plans. You can change plans at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

| Plan | Best for | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Side projects and evaluation | Up to 3 |
| Team | Growing product teams | Up to 25 |
| Enterprise | Scale, SSO and SLAs | Unlimited |

## Upgrade or downgrade

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Select **Change plan**.
3. Confirm — upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades at the end of the cycle.

:::info Proration
When you upgrade mid‑cycle, we prorate the difference automatically so you only pay for
what you use.
:::
