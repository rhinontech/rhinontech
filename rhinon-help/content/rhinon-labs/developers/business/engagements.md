"How we help" in one line: **one team takes the whole thing** - discovery, design, build, deploy,
support - so the client never coordinates hand-offs. This page maps the models we sell to how we
actually deliver, and tracks the engagements themselves.

## The models we sell

The public framing is in [Engagement models](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/engagement-models);
internally, each maps to a different revenue shape:

| Model | Best for | Revenue shape | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed project** | A defined outcome - MVP, site, specific build | One-off, scoped up front | Scope creep; convert to retainer at launch |
| **Dedicated team** | Ongoing capacity, evolving scope | Recurring while allocated | Utilisation; keep allocation explicit |
| **Support retainer** | Post-launch care and expansion | Recurring monthly/quarterly | The natural upsell after every fixed project |

The healthiest path is the chain: **fixed project → support retainer → expansion work**. Every
launch conversation should include the retainer.

## The delivery flow behind the pitch

What the client hears as [How we work](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/how-we-work) runs
internally as:

1. **Intake** - the prospect messages WhatsApp (**+91 82492 91789**) or `hello@rhinon.tech`. We
   qualify against [who it's for](/rhinon-labs/developers/business/target-audience) before booking
   anything.
2. **Free discovery call** - understand the business, the bottleneck or the idea. No pitch deck;
   questions.
3. **Strategy sprint** - a written plan (scope, approach, model, price) before any build starts.
   This is where the engagement model is chosen.
4. **Build & connect** - delivery per the
   [engineering handbook](/rhinon-labs/developers/introduction).
5. **Deploy & enablement** - launch, training, documented handover -
   [everything the client owns](/rhinon-labs/developers/local-development).
6. **Retainer** - propose ongoing support at handover, while trust is highest.

## In-house engagements

The products we build for ourselves are engagements too - they consume the same capacity and serve
as our strongest proof of work:

- **Saleszium** - SaaS platform, built and operated in-house. Ongoing.
- **Furrcircle** - consumer mobile app, built and operated in-house. Ongoing.
- **Rhinon ecosystem** - rhinonlabs.com, rhinon.tech, this docs hub, and the shared backend
  (`api.rhinontech.in`).
