## The funnel

1. **Discover** - search or referral lands on rhinonlabs.com or the public guide.
2. **Trust** - service pages, the flagship products, and (eventually) real case studies.
3. **Contact** - one WhatsApp message. Deliberately not a long form.
4. **Qualify & convert** - per the intake flow in
   [Engagements](/rhinon-labs/developers/business/engagements).

## Messaging pillars

Use these consistently - every page, proposal and post should ladder up to one of them:

- **From idea to production - and from chaos to clarity.** The umbrella hook.
- **Scale your operations, not your headcount.** The Automate pillar's promise.
- **One partner, end to end.** Design → build → deploy → support; no hand-offs that drop.
- **You own everything.** Code, data, infrastructure - documented at handover.
- **We run our own products.** Not just an agency - a house of products that also builds for
  clients.

Tone: plain, confident, no agency jargon. Claims must be backed by the
[client roster](/rhinon-labs/developers/business/clients) - the public metrics are currently
flagged as unverified there.
