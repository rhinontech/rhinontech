## Where Rhinon Labs sits

Rhinon Tech is a **house of products** - it designs, builds and operates its own technology
in-house. Three ventures carry the flag today:

| Venture | What it is | Role in the market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Saleszium** | All-in-one customer engagement & sales platform | Our SaaS proof point - shows we can build and operate a real product |
| **Furrcircle** | India's all-in-one app for pet parents | Our consumer proof point - mobile, community, scale |
| **Rhinon Labs** | Founder-led, full-stack product studio | The client-facing arm - takes on **select** client work |

The parent company does not do agency work; **all external engagements flow through Rhinon Labs**.
That separation is deliberate: it lets the studio point at products we built and *run* - not just
shipped - as evidence.

## The market hook

> **From idea to production - and from chaos to clarity.**

Two pillars, one studio:

- **Build** - complete digital products, design through deployment, any stack (mainly
  JavaScript/TypeScript and Python).
- **Automate** - workflow automation, AI agents and dashboards that scale operations, not headcount.

The full public case lives in [Why Rhinon Labs](/rhinon-labs/guide/why-rhinon-labs); the internal
read on who buys it is in [Target audience](/rhinon-labs/developers/business/target-audience).
