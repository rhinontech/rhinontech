The public version is [Who it's for](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/who-its-for). This is the
internal read: the segments we actually pursue, what makes them buy, and how to qualify them at
intake.

## Segments

### 1. Non-technical founders

- **Who:** founders with an idea and no technical team - often first-time, often pre-funding.
- **Pain:** can't ship without a technical co-founder; burned by freelancers who deliver a piece,
  not a product.
- **Trigger:** "I have an idea / a deck / early interest, and nothing built."
- **Lead with:** [MVP development](/rhinon-labs/guide/product-engineering/mvp-development) and the
  end-to-end promise - *you don't need a technical co-founder to ship*.
- **Objections:** cost vs. hiring; "will I own the code?" (answer: fully - it's in every doc);
  "what happens after launch?" (retainer).

### 2. SMB & ops leaders drowning in manual work

- **Who:** small and scaling businesses whose operations have outgrown spreadsheets - the
  **Automate** pillar's core buyer. Overlaps with Saleszium's audience (useful cross-signal).
- **Pain:** hours lost to copy-paste work, scattered data, tools that don't talk to each other.
- **Trigger:** "we're about to hire someone just to manage this."
- **Lead with:** [workflow automation](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/workflow-automation),
  [dashboards](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/internal-dashboards) and *scale operations, not
  headcount*.
- **Objections:** "we tried Zapier"; answer with systems-not-duct-tape - real logic, resilient,
  connected to their data.

### 3. Product & engineering leaders needing capacity

- **Who:** teams with a roadmap bigger than their headcount.
- **Pain:** hiring is slow and permanent; agencies deliver outside their process.
- **Trigger:** a deadline the current team can't hit.
- **Lead with:** [dedicated teams](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/dedicated-teams) - embedded
  people backed by a full studio.
- **Objections:** integration friction; answer: we work inside *their* process and tools.

### 4. Established businesses adding AI

- **Who:** companies with a working product or operation who want AI in it - the segment with the
  strongest current tailwind.
- **Pain:** pressure to "do AI" without knowing what's real; data sensitivity.
- **Trigger:** board/competitor pressure, or a repetitive knowledge task that obviously should be
  automated.
- **Lead with:** [generative AI integrations](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/generative-ai-integrations),
  [AI agents](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/ai-agents-and-assistants), and **private LLM
  deployments** for the data-sensitive.
- **Objections:** hallucination and privacy; answer: grounded RAG on their content, guardrails,
  private deployments.

## Qualifying at intake

Green flags (from the public "signs we're a fit", used as an actual checklist):

- Has an idea but **no technical team** - or a team losing hours to **repetitive manual work**.
- Data **scattered** across disconnected tools.
- About to **hire for work that should be automated**.
- Wants one partner for **design, build, deploy and support** - not one slice.

Red flags: shopping for the cheapest hourly rate; wants staff without any ownership of outcomes;
scope with no decision-maker attached.
