We build **custom AI agents** that work inside your daily operations - drafting replies, organising
information and handling routine tasks - so your team focuses on the work that needs a human.

## What it is

Smart AI tools embedded in your workflow rather than a chatbot you have to visit: they read your
data, take actions, and run quietly in the background.

## What we build

- **Email and message triage** - sort, prioritise and draft responses
- **Lead research and enrichment** - gather and organise prospect data
- **Document extraction** - pull structured data out of PDFs and forms
- **Summarisation agents** - turn long threads and documents into briefs
- **Appointment and follow-up setters** - keep things moving automatically

## Our approach

We design agents around your real processes and data, connect them securely to your tools, and -
where privacy matters - can use **private LLM deployments** so your data stays under your control.

## Outcomes

- Hours of routine work handled automatically
- Faster responses and fewer dropped balls
- AI leverage without changing how your team works

We build agents as part of a [cohesive system](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/workflow-automation)
- connected to your data and dashboards - not as a novelty bolted on top.
