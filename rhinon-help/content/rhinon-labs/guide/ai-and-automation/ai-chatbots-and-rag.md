We build **AI chatbots and knowledge assistants** that answer from *your* content - your docs,
policies and data - so the answers are accurate, not generic guesses.

## What it is

A conversational assistant grounded in your knowledge base using **RAG** (retrieval-augmented
generation): for customer support, sales, or internal "ask the company anything" tools.

## What's included

- Ingesting your documents, sites and data into a knowledge base
- Retrieval-augmented answering for grounded, accurate responses
- Customisable tone, branding and guardrails
- Human handoff and escalation where needed
- Deployment to your site, app or internal tools

## Our approach

We connect leading LLMs (or **private deployments** for sensitive data) to your content with a
retrieval layer, so the bot cites your knowledge and stays on-topic.

## Outcomes

- 24/7 instant answers for customers or staff
- Less repetitive load on your team
- Answers that reflect your actual content and policies
