Good dashboards and AI need good data underneath. We build the **pipelines and data foundations**
that move, clean and unify your data so everything downstream just works.

## What it is

The plumbing of your data: pipelines that collect data from your tools, clean and transform it, and
land it somewhere your dashboards, models and reports can use.

## What's included

- Data integration across your tools and sources
- Cleaning, transformation and validation
- Centralised data storage (warehouse/database)
- Scheduled or real-time syncing
- Foundations for analytics and AI

## Our approach & stack

We build pipelines primarily in **Python** and SQL, with cloud data stores, designed for
reliability and full **data ownership** - your data stays yours.

## Outcomes

- One trustworthy, unified view of your data
- Dashboards and AI that run on clean inputs
- Less manual exporting and reconciling
