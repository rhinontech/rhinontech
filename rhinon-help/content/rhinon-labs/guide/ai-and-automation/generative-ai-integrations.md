Already have a product? We **add generative-AI features** to it - the kind that make users say "how
did it do that?" - built responsibly and integrated cleanly.

## What it is

LLM-powered capabilities embedded into your existing app or workflow: generation, summarisation,
classification, extraction, semantic search and more.

## What we build

- **Content generation** - drafts, descriptions, replies
- **Summarisation** - long content into short, useful briefs
- **Classification & tagging** - auto-organise data and tickets
- **Semantic search** - find by meaning, not just keywords
- **Extraction** - structured data from unstructured text

## Our approach & stack

We integrate models from providers like **Claude/Anthropic**, **OpenAI** and others - or **private
models** for data-sensitive use cases - with the right prompting, evaluation and guardrails so the
feature is reliable, not flaky.

## Outcomes

- A differentiated, AI-powered product
- Real productivity gains for your users
- Responsible, controlled AI behaviour

## Why us

We integrate AI as a dependable product feature - with attention to accuracy, cost and privacy -
not a demo that breaks in front of users.
