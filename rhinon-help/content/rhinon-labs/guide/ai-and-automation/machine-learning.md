When your data can predict or recommend, we help you put it to work. We build **machine-learning
and data-science solutions** - and, crucially, deploy them into your product where they create value.

## What it is

Custom models and data-science work: predictions, recommendations, scoring, forecasting, anomaly
detection and analysis - built on your data.

## What's included

- Problem framing and data assessment
- Feature engineering and model development
- Evaluation and validation
- Deployment into your product or workflow
- Monitoring and iteration

## Our approach & stack

We work in the **Python** data ecosystem (pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow where needed),
and - just as importantly - we ship models to production rather than leaving them in a notebook.

## Outcomes

- Predictions and recommendations that drive decisions
- Insight pulled out of data you already have
- Models running live, not stuck in research

and [products](/rhinon-labs/guide/product-engineering/web-app-development), so the value actually
