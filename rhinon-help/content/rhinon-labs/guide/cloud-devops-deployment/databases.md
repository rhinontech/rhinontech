Your data is the heart of your product. We design and manage **databases** that are well-structured,
fast and dependable - so your product stays quick and your data stays trustworthy.

## What it is

The design, optimisation and care of the database layer: schema design, indexing, migrations,
backups and performance tuning.

## What's included

- Schema and data modelling
- SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL options
- Indexing and query optimisation
- Migrations and versioning
- Backups and data integrity

## Our approach

We model data around how your product actually uses it, tune for performance, and put backups and
safeguards in place so your data is protected.

## Outcomes

- Fast queries even as data grows
- A consistent, well-structured data layer
- Safe migrations and reliable backups
