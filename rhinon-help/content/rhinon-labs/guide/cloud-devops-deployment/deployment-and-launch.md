The last mile is where many projects stall. We handle **deployment and launch** end to end, so your
product goes live smoothly and stays up.

## What it is

Everything between "it works on our machine" and "it's live for customers": environment setup,
release, domain and SSL, monitoring, and launch-day support.

## What's included

- Production environment setup
- Domain, SSL and configuration
- Final testing and a launch checklist
- Monitoring and error tracking
- Launch-day support

## Our approach

We treat launch as a planned, tested event - not a leap of faith - so go-live is calm and your
product is observable from minute one.

## Outcomes

- A smooth, on-time launch
- A monitored, stable production system
- Confidence that issues are caught early

## Why us

Because we build, deploy and [support](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/maintenance-and-support)
the same product, launch is just one step in a continuous process - not a risky hand-off.
