Trust is hard to earn and easy to lose. We build **security and data protection** into your product
from the start - and make sure you fully own your data.

## What it is

The practices and safeguards that protect your product and your users: secure authentication,
encryption, access control and responsible data handling.

## What's included

- Secure authentication and authorisation
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Role-based access control
- Secure API and integration practices
- Data ownership and privacy by design

## Our approach

We follow security best practices throughout the build, and - for sensitive use cases - can use
**private LLM deployments** and isolated infrastructure so your data never leaves your control.

## Outcomes

- A product users and partners can trust
- Reduced risk of breaches and data loss
- Full ownership and control of your data
