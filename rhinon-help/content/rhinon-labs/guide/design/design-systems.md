As products grow, consistency gets hard. A **design system** keeps everything coherent and makes
every future feature faster to design and build.

## What it is

A reusable library of components, patterns and rules - the single source of truth for how your
product looks and behaves.

## What's included

- Component and pattern library (in Figma)
- Design tokens (colour, spacing, typography)
- Usage guidelines and documentation
- Alignment between design and coded components

## Our approach

We build the system to match your real product needs and keep design and code in lockstep, so a
component looks and behaves the same in Figma and in the app.

## Outcomes

- A consistent experience across your product
- Faster design and development of new features
- Easier onboarding for new team members
