Before you invest in building, it pays to *feel* the product. We create **interactive prototypes**
that let you test ideas with real users and stakeholders early.

## What it is

A clickable, realistic simulation of your product or feature - enough to experience the flow and
gather feedback without writing production code.

## What's included

- Interactive, clickable prototypes
- Realistic flows and states
- User-testing-ready deliverables
- A clear path from prototype to build

## Our approach

We prototype in **Figma**, focusing on the flows that matter most, so you can validate direction and
de-risk decisions before development starts.

## Outcomes

- Early validation and feedback
- Fewer expensive changes mid-build
- Confident, evidence-based decisions

## Why us

Our prototypes feed straight into the real build - what you test is what we engineer. Pairs well
with [MVP development](/rhinon-labs/guide/product-engineering/mvp-development).
