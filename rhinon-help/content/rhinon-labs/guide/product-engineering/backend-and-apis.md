Behind every great product is a backend that just works. We build **reliable, secure server-side
systems and APIs** that scale with you.

## What it is

The engine room of your product: business logic, data, authentication and the APIs that connect
your apps, services and partners.

## What's included

- REST and/or GraphQL API design and development
- Authentication, authorisation and security
- Database modelling and data access layers
- Third-party and internal integrations
- Documentation and versioning

## Our approach & stack

We build backends in **Node.js** and **Python (FastAPI, Django, Flask)**, with **PostgreSQL**,
**MySQL** or **NoSQL** databases, designed for reliability, security and clean scaling.

## Outcomes

- APIs that are easy to consume and built to last
- A secure, well-structured data layer
- Systems that integrate cleanly with the rest of your stack
