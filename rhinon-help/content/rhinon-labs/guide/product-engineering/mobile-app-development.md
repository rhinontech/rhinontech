We build **mobile apps** that feel native, ship to both app stores, and share a single, efficient
codebase - so you reach everyone without doubling the cost.

## What it is

A mobile application for **iOS and Android**: consumer apps, companion apps for your platform, or
internal tools for teams on the move.

## What's included

- Cross-platform app design and development
- Backend and API integration
- Push notifications and real-time features
- App Store and Play Store release support
- Updates and maintenance

## Our approach & stack

We typically build with **React Native / Expo** for a single cross-platform codebase, backed by the
same **Node.js / Python** services that power your web product - so everything stays in sync.

## Outcomes

- One app, both platforms, lower cost
- A consistent experience across web and mobile
- A smooth path through app store review and release

## Why us

Because we also build your [backend](/rhinon-labs/guide/product-engineering/backend-and-apis) and
[web app](/rhinon-labs/guide/product-engineering/web-app-development), your mobile app shares data
and logic instead of reinventing it.
