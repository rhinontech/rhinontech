Launch isn't the finish line. We offer **ongoing maintenance and support** so your product stays
secure, up to date and improving long after it goes live.

## What it is

Continuous care for your live product: monitoring, bug fixes, updates, small enhancements and the
peace of mind that someone's watching.

## What's included

- Monitoring and uptime checks
- Bug fixes and issue resolution
- Dependency and security updates
- Small feature enhancements
- Optional monthly or quarterly retainer

## Our approach

We offer a flexible **support retainer** after deployment so we can optimise and expand your systems
as your business grows - you're never left stranded with software no one maintains.

## Outcomes

- A product that stays reliable and secure
- Fast response when something needs attention
- Continuous improvement over time
