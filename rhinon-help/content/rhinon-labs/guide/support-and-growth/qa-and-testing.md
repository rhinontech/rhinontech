Quality isn't an accident. We build in **QA and testing** so your product works as intended - and
keeps working as it changes.

## What it is

The processes and automated tests that catch issues before your users do: functional testing,
automated test suites, and quality checks across the product.

## What's included

- Functional and end-to-end testing
- Automated test suites
- Cross-browser and cross-device checks
- Regression testing on changes
- Bug tracking and resolution

## Our approach

We test throughout the build, automate the checks that matter, and run regression tests on every
change so new work doesn't break existing features.

## Outcomes

- Fewer bugs reaching production
- Confidence to ship changes quickly
- A consistent experience across devices

## Why us

We pair testing with our [CI/CD pipelines](/rhinon-labs/guide/cloud-devops-deployment/devops-and-cicd)
so quality checks run automatically on every release.
