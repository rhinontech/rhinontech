Sometimes you need a clear head and honest advice before (or instead of) a build. Our **technical
consulting** helps you make the right decisions about technology, architecture and product strategy.

## What it is

Expert guidance from people who build for a living: architecture reviews, tech-stack decisions,
feasibility assessments and product/technical strategy.

## What's included

- Architecture and code reviews
- Technology and stack recommendations
- Feasibility and effort assessments
- Product and technical strategy
- A clear, actionable plan

## Our approach

We start by understanding your goals and constraints, then give you honest, practical guidance - even
when that means "you don't need to build this yet."

## Outcomes

- Confident, well-informed decisions
- A clear technical direction
- Avoided costly mistakes
