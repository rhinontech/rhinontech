There's no one-size-fits-all way to work together. We offer a few **engagement models** so you can
pick what fits your goals, timeline and budget.

## 1. Fixed project

Best when you have a defined outcome - an MVP, a website, a specific build.

- Clear scope, timeline and deliverables
- A defined plan agreed up front
- Ideal for launching something specific

## 2. Dedicated team

Best when you need ongoing capacity or your scope will evolve.

- Designers and engineers dedicated to you
- Works as an extension of your team
- Scale up or down as your roadmap changes

See [Dedicated teams](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/dedicated-teams).

## 3. Support retainer

Best after launch, to keep things running and improving.

- Monthly or quarterly plan
- Maintenance, monitoring and enhancements
- Optimise and expand your systems over time

See [Maintenance & support](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/maintenance-and-support).

## Not sure which fits?

Most engagements start with a [discovery call](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/how-we-work) - we'll
recommend the model that makes the most sense for where you are.
