## Scope & services

**What does Rhinon Labs actually do?**
We're a full-stack product studio. We take ideas from design through development, deployment and
support - web, mobile, SaaS, AI, automation, dashboards and more. Browse the service groups in the
sidebar for the full list.

**Can you build my whole product, end to end?**
Yes. We handle the entire technical side - design, build, deploy and maintain - so you don't need an
in-house engineering team to ship. See [How we work](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/how-we-work).

**I'm not technical. Can we still work together?**
Absolutely. Many of our clients are non-technical founders. We translate your idea into a real
product and handle the technical decisions.

## Technology

**What technologies do you use?**
Mainly **JavaScript/TypeScript** (React, Next.js, Node) and **Python** (FastAPI, Django, data/ML),
with AWS cloud infrastructure. See our [tech stack](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/tech-stack).

**Do you work with my preferred stack / existing tools?**
Usually yes - we choose technology to fit your needs and integrate with the tools you already use.
Tell us your constraints and we'll work with them.

**Can you add AI to my existing product?**
Yes - see [Generative AI integrations](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/generative-ai-integrations)
and [AI agents](/rhinon-labs/guide/ai-and-automation/ai-agents-and-assistants).

## Ownership & process

**Who owns the code and data?**
You do - fully. We hand over documented code, data and infrastructure that belong to you.

**How do projects start?**
With a quick conversation, then a discovery call and a clear plan before we build. See
[How we work](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/how-we-work).

## Pricing & support

**How much does it cost?**
Engagements are scoped to your needs. We discuss pricing once we understand the work - and we offer
flexible [engagement models](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/engagement-models).

**Do you maintain what you build?**
Yes - we offer an optional
[support retainer](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/maintenance-and-support) to keep your
product running and improving after launch.
