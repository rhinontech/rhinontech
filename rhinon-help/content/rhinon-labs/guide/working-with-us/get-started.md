The fastest way to start is a quick message. Tell us the **idea you want to build** or the
**bottleneck you want to fix**, and we'll tell you how we'd approach it.

## Message us on WhatsApp

👉 **[Chat with us on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/918249291789)** - **+91 82492 91789**

We'll reply, ask a few questions, and if it looks like a fit, set up a short discovery call to plan
your project.

## What to tell us

The more concrete you can be, the faster we can help:

- **What** you want to build, or which manual work is slowing you down
- **Who** it's for (customers, your team, both)
- Any **tools** you already use that it should work with
- Your rough **timeline** and goals

## What happens next

1. **You message us** on WhatsApp with the basics.
2. **We reply** and set up a free discovery call.
3. **We map a plan** - scope, approach and the right
   [engagement model](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/engagement-models).
4. **We build, deploy and support** - see
   [How we work](/rhinon-labs/guide/working-with-us/how-we-work).

:::tip No commitment to start
The first chat is free and there's no obligation. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.
:::
