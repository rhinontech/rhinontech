Working with Rhinon Labs is hands-on and clear. We get to the root of the problem, build the right
thing, and stay with you after it ships.

## Our process

### 1. Free discovery call
We start by understanding how your business runs today - where time is lost, where things feel
messy, and what's slowing your team or blocking your idea.

### 2. Strategy sprint
We map out exactly what to build - the product, systems and automations needed - and give you a
clear plan *before* anything is built.

### 3. Build & connect
We design and build your product, dashboards, automations and AI tools, and connect everything to
your existing systems and data.

### 4. Deploy & enablement
We deploy the system, train your team, and make sure everything runs smoothly - then support and
expand it as you grow.

## What to expect

- A **founder-led**, hands-on engagement - you work directly with the people doing the work.
- Solutions built around **your** processes and goals, not a template.
- **Full ownership**: your code, data and infrastructure, documented at handover.
- A focus on **outcomes**: shipped products, time saved, real visibility.

## After launch

We offer an optional [support retainer](/rhinon-labs/guide/support-and-growth/maintenance-and-support)
so we can maintain, optimise and expand your systems as your business grows.
