We're **stack-flexible by design**. We work mainly with **JavaScript/TypeScript** and **Python**,
and we choose the right tools for your problem rather than forcing every project into the same mould.

## Frontend

- **React** and **Next.js**
- **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS** and modern styling
- Accessible, performant, responsive interfaces

## Backend & APIs

- **Node.js** (Express, Nest)
- **Python** (FastAPI, Django, Flask)
- REST and GraphQL APIs
- Secure authentication and integrations

## Mobile

- **React Native / Expo** for cross-platform iOS + Android

## AI & data

- LLM integrations (Claude/Anthropic, OpenAI) and **private model deployments**
- RAG / knowledge assistants
- **Python** data and ML ecosystem (pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch/TensorFlow)
- Data pipelines and warehousing

## Cloud, DevOps & data stores

- **AWS** and cloud-native infrastructure
- CI/CD pipelines and containerisation (Docker)
- **PostgreSQL**, **MySQL** and NoSQL databases

## How we choose

We pick technology based on your requirements, scale and team - favouring proven, well-supported
tools so your product is maintainable long after launch. If your idea needs a stack we haven't
listed, we'll tell you honestly whether it's the right fit.
