## Getting started

**Do I need technical skills to set up Saleszium?**
No. Training the chatbot and configuring channels is done through the interface. The only step
that may need a hand is pasting the widget snippet onto your website - and most site builders make
that a one-time copy-paste.

**How long does setup take?**
You can have a trained chatbot live in about ten minutes with the
[Quickstart](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/quickstart). Connecting channels and building out
your CRM happens over time.

## The chatbot

**Where does the chatbot get its answers?**
From your [knowledge base](/saleszium/guide/features/knowledge-base) - the websites, documents and
articles you train it on. It pulls the most relevant pieces into each reply.

**What if the bot can't answer?**
It can hand the conversation to a human in your [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox).
Add the missing information to your knowledge base and re-train so it can handle it next time.

**Can it speak more than one language?**
The chatbot can be trained from your content and handle conversational questions. Confirm specific
language support for your use case in your account.

## Channels & CRM

**Which channels can I connect?**
Website chat, [WhatsApp](/saleszium/guide/features/whatsapp) and
[email](/saleszium/guide/features/email), all flowing into one inbox.

**Do conversations become CRM records automatically?**
Yes. Chats, form submissions and emails create or update
[contacts and leads](/saleszium/guide/features/crm) without manual data entry.

## Plans & billing

**What are AI tokens?**
The unit of AI usage consumed as the bot reads and writes. Each plan includes a monthly allowance -
see [Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).

**Can I change plans later?**
Yes - upgrade or change your plan from **Settings → Billing** as your needs grow.

## Team

**How many people can use Saleszium?**
That depends on your plan; higher tiers allow more (or unlimited) members. See
[Team & roles](/saleszium/guide/features/team-and-roles).
