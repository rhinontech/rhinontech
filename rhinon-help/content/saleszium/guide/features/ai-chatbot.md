The **AI Chatbot** is the front door to your business. It greets visitors, answers their
questions instantly using *your* content, captures their details, and brings in a human when the
conversation needs one.

## What it does

- **Answers from your knowledge** - the bot replies using your [knowledge base](/saleszium/guide/features/knowledge-base),
  so answers reflect your products, policies and pricing rather than generic guesses.
- **Stays in context** - it remembers the thread of a conversation across multiple questions.
- **Works around the clock** - it covers nights, weekends and time zones without staffing.
- **Knows when to escalate** - it can hand a conversation to a live agent in your
  [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox).

## Why it matters

Most visitors want a fast answer, not a form. A trained chatbot deflects repetitive questions,
keeps people engaged, and turns anonymous traffic into identified leads - while your team focuses
on the conversations that need a person.

## How to use it

1. Build a [knowledge base](/saleszium/guide/features/knowledge-base) from your website, files or
   articles.
2. **Train** the bot on those sources.
3. **Customise** its name, avatar, colours, welcome message and suggested questions.
4. **Install** the [widget](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/install-the-widget) on your site.
5. Monitor conversations and keep improving the knowledge base over time.

## Add-ons and extras

- **Voice bot** - voice-based interactions (available as an add-on).
- **Direct calling** - start a call directly from the chat experience.

:::tip Better knowledge, better bot
The single biggest lever on answer quality is your knowledge base. If the bot gives a weak
answer, add or clarify a source and re-train.
:::
