**Campaigns** let you reach many people at once with a coordinated message, then measure the
response. They're how you turn your CRM into active outreach.

## Types of campaign

- **Email campaigns** - send to a list or segment, using templates and personalisation.
- **Chatbot campaigns** - run targeted interactions through your chatbot.
- **LinkedIn campaigns** - connect a LinkedIn account to run social outreach and posting.

## What it does

- **Audience targeting** - send to segments built from your [CRM](/saleszium/guide/features/crm).
- **Scheduling** - choose when a campaign goes out.
- **Performance tracking** - see opens, clicks and responses to learn what works.

## Why it matters

Campaigns keep leads warm and customers engaged without one-by-one effort. Tracking results means
each send teaches you something for the next one.

## How to use it

1. Open **Campaigns** and create a new campaign.
2. Pick the **type** (email, chatbot or LinkedIn) and your **audience**.
3. Write the **message** - use a template and personalise it.
4. **Schedule** or send it.
5. Review **performance** and refine.

For a full email example, see
[Launch an email campaign](/saleszium/guide/how-to/launch-an-email-campaign).

:::info Plan limits apply
The number of campaigns you can run is set by your plan - see
[Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).
:::
