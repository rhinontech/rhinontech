The **CRM** is where the people you talk to become records you can manage. Leads captured by your
chatbot, forms and inbox flow straight in - no copy-paste, no spreadsheets.

## The core records

- **Leads** - potential customers you're working to win.
- **Contacts** - individual people, with their details and full interaction history.
- **Companies** - the organisations contacts belong to, so you can see the whole account.
- **Groups / segments** - collections you can target and report on.

## What it does

- **Captures automatically** - a chatbot conversation, form submission or inbound email creates
  or updates a record.
- **Centralises history** - every conversation, ticket and deal sits on the contact's timeline.
- **Qualifies leads** - track and prioritise who's worth your team's time.
- **Feeds the pipeline** - promote a lead into a [deal](/saleszium/guide/features/sales-pipeline)
  when it's real.

## Why it matters

When your CRM fills itself from real conversations, your data stays current and your team spends
time selling instead of admin. Everyone sees the same, up-to-date view of each customer.

## How to use it

1. Open **CRM** to see your leads, contacts and companies.
2. Open any record to view its **timeline** - conversations, emails, tickets and deals.
3. **Assign** records to teammates and add notes.
4. Move qualified leads into your [pipeline](/saleszium/guide/features/sales-pipeline).

:::tip Let automation do the busywork
Use [workflows](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation) to assign new leads, add them to a
segment, or trigger a follow-up the moment they're created.
:::
