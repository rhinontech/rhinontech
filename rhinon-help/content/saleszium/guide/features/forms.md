**Forms** are the simplest way to capture leads. Embed one on your website, and every submission
flows straight into your [CRM](/saleszium/guide/features/crm).

## What it does

- **Lead capture** - collect names, emails and any details you need.
- **Straight into the CRM** - submissions create or update contacts and leads automatically.
- **Automation triggers** - a submission can kick off a [workflow](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation).

## Why it matters

Forms turn interest into a record you can act on. Because they feed the CRM and automations
directly, a new lead can be assigned, emailed and followed up the instant they hit submit.

## How to use it

1. Open **Forms** and create a new form.
2. Add the **fields** you want to collect.
3. **Embed** it on your website, or use it in a campaign.
4. Connect a [workflow](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation) to act on submissions.

See [Capture leads with forms](/saleszium/guide/how-to/capture-leads-with-forms) for a walkthrough.

:::tip Keep forms short
Ask only for what you need right now. Shorter forms convert better - you can enrich the record
later through conversations.
:::
