The **Sales Pipeline** turns your leads into a clear, visual picture of opportunities - what's in
play, what's close, and what's at risk.

## What it does

- **Stages** - see deals move through steps like Prospecting → Qualified → Negotiation → Closed.
- **Deals** - each opportunity carries a value, a stage and a probability of closing.
- **Forecasting** - estimate expected revenue based on deal values and probabilities.
- **Ownership** - assign deals to team members and track their progress.

## Why it matters

A pipeline answers the questions every sales team asks: *Where are we? What's likely to close
this month? What needs attention?* It replaces guesswork and scattered notes with one shared view.

## How to use it

1. Open **Pipeline** (or **Deals**).
2. **Create a deal** from a qualified [lead](/saleszium/guide/features/crm) - set its value and stage.
3. **Drag** deals between stages as they progress.
4. Review the **forecast** to see expected revenue.
5. Spot stalled deals and follow up.

:::tip Customise your stages
Match the pipeline stages to *your* sales process. The closer the stages mirror how you actually
sell, the more useful the forecast.
:::
