Saleszium includes **SEO & Performance Audits** so you can keep the website that feeds your
funnel fast and search-friendly - without leaving the platform.

## What it does

- **Performance audits** - measure page speed and performance scores.
- **SEO checks** - review pages against SEO and accessibility best practices.
- **Recommendations** - get pointers on what to improve.
- **Tracking** - watch scores change over time as you make fixes.

## Why it matters

Slow or poorly-optimised pages quietly cost you traffic and conversions. Built-in audits mean you
can catch issues early and connect site health to the leads and conversations it drives.

## How to use it

1. Open the **SEO / Performance** area.
2. **Scan** a page or your site.
3. Review the **scores and recommendations**.
4. Make improvements and **re-scan** to confirm progress.

:::info Scan limits by plan
How often you can run scans depends on your plan (for example, a set number of scans per week).
See [Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).
:::
