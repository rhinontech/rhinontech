Saleszium is built for teams. **Team & Roles** is where you bring people in and decide what each
person can see and do.

## What it does

- **Members** - invite teammates to your workspace.
- **Role-based access** - grant permissions by role so people see only what's relevant.
- **Collaboration** - assign conversations, leads, deals and tickets to specific people.

## Typical roles

- **Owner / Super admin** - full access, including billing and settings.
- **Admin / Manager** - full day-to-day access, usually without billing.
- **Agent / Support** - focused on conversations, chats and tickets.

Exact roles and permissions can vary by plan and configuration.

## Why it matters

As your team grows, not everyone should touch everything. Roles keep sensitive areas (like
billing) restricted while letting agents move fast on the work that's theirs.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. **Invite** members by email and assign a **role**.
3. Adjust roles as responsibilities change.
4. **Assign** records and conversations so ownership is always clear.

:::info Members are plan-limited
The number of members you can add depends on your plan. See
[Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).
:::
