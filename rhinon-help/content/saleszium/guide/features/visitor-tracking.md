**Live Visitor Tracking** shows you who is on your website *right now* - what pages they're
viewing and how engaged they are - so you can reach out at exactly the right moment.

## What it does

- **Real-time presence** - see current visitors and how many are online.
- **Page activity** - watch which pages people are browsing.
- **Proactive engagement** - start a chat or offer help based on what they're doing.
- **Session insight** - understand behaviour, sessions and engagement over time.

## Why it matters

A visitor on your pricing page is a buying signal. Seeing live activity lets your team (or an
automation) engage high-intent visitors before they leave - turning passive traffic into
conversations.

## How to use it

1. Make sure the [widget](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/install-the-widget) is installed.
2. Open **Live visitors** (traffic) to see who's online.
3. Watch the pages a visitor is viewing.
4. **Engage** them with a proactive message, or let a
   [workflow](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation) trigger one.

See [Engage live visitors](/saleszium/guide/how-to/engage-live-visitors) for tactics.

:::tip Engage on intent, not on arrival
Reaching out the second someone lands can feel pushy. Trigger engagement on *intent* signals -
time on a key page, or a return visit.
:::
