Saleszium connects to **WhatsApp Business** so you can meet customers on the channel they use
every day - and manage it from the same [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox) as
everything else.

## What it does

- **Two-way messaging** - receive customer messages and reply, all inside Saleszium.
- **One inbox** - WhatsApp threads sit alongside website chat and email.
- **Customer records** - a WhatsApp conversation links to the contact's profile in your CRM.

## Why it matters

WhatsApp has extremely high open and response rates. Handling it inside Saleszium means your team
isn't juggling a separate phone, and every conversation is captured against the right customer.

## How to use it

1. Open **Settings → Channels** and choose **WhatsApp**.
2. Connect your **WhatsApp Business** number by following the guided steps.
3. Once connected, incoming messages appear in your inbox and you can reply directly.

For a detailed walkthrough, see
[Connect WhatsApp](/saleszium/guide/how-to/connect-whatsapp).

:::info Business account required
WhatsApp messaging runs on the WhatsApp Business platform. You'll need an eligible business
number to connect. Availability and messaging rules follow WhatsApp's own policies.
:::

:::tip Notifications
Turn on notifications so your team sees new WhatsApp messages the moment they arrive.
:::
