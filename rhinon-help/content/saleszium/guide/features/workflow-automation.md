**Workflow Automation** is how Saleszium does the repetitive work for you. You define the
rules once, and the platform handles follow-ups, assignments, tasks and messages automatically.

## The building blocks

Every workflow is made of three parts:

1. **Trigger** - the event that starts it. For example: *a form is submitted*, *a new lead is
   created*, or *a deal changes stage*.
2. **Conditions** *(optional)* - rules that must be true to continue. For example: *only if the
   lead came from the pricing page*.
3. **Actions** - what happens. For example: *send an email*, *assign to a teammate*, *create a
   task*, or *add a delay before the next step*.

## What you can automate

- **Lead routing** - assign new leads to the right person automatically.
- **Follow-ups** - send a message a set time after an event.
- **Task creation** - generate a to-do for your team when something needs a human.
- **Multi-step sequences** - chain actions with delays into a nurturing flow.

## Why it matters

Manual follow-up is where deals and tickets go to die. Automation makes sure every lead gets a
timely response and every routine step happens consistently - without anyone remembering to do it.

## How to use it

1. Open **Automation / Workflows**.
2. Create a workflow and choose a **trigger**.
3. Add **conditions** to narrow when it runs.
4. Add one or more **actions**, including delays for multi-step flows.
5. **Activate** it and monitor how it performs.

See [Build a workflow](/saleszium/guide/how-to/build-a-workflow) for a worked example.
