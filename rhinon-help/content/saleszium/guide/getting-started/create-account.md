Your Saleszium journey starts with an **account** and an **organisation** (your workspace). The
organisation is the container for everything - your chatbot, conversations, CRM data, team
members and billing all belong to it.

## Sign up

1. Go to the Saleszium sign-up page and register with your work email.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Create your **organisation** - give it your company name. This becomes your workspace.

## What gets created

When your organisation is ready, Saleszium sets up:

- An empty **inbox** for incoming conversations.
- A blank **knowledge base** to train your chatbot.
- A starter **CRM** ready for leads, contacts and companies.
- Your profile as the first **member**, with the highest level of access.

## A note on plans

New organisations start on a trial with **Starter** limits. You can review what each plan
includes - AI usage, number of team members, training sources and more - on the
[Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing) page.

:::tip Invite your team early
You don't have to do everything yourself. Once your workspace exists, head to
[Workspace & team](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/workspace-and-team) to add colleagues and
assign roles.
:::
