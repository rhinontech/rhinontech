The **widget** is the chat bubble that appears on your website. It's how visitors reach your
chatbot and your live agents, and how Saleszium sees who is browsing your site.

## How installation works

Saleszium gives you a small snippet to add to your website. You paste it once, and the widget
appears on every page.

1. Open **Settings → Installation** (or the **Widget** / **Channels** area).
2. Copy your unique widget snippet.
3. Paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag - or add it through
   your site builder / tag manager.
4. Publish your site and refresh - the chat bubble should appear.

:::tip Using a site builder or CMS?
Most platforms (Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, etc.) have a place to add custom code or a
"header/footer scripts" setting. Paste the snippet there once and it applies site-wide.
:::

## Customise the widget

Before or after installing, you can style the widget to match your brand:

- **Name and avatar** for the chatbot.
- **Colours** and position (which corner it sits in).
- A **welcome message** and suggested questions.

## Confirm it's working

- The chat bubble appears on your live site.
- Opening it starts a conversation with your chatbot.
- New visitors show up in [Live visitor tracking](/saleszium/guide/features/visitor-tracking).

:::warning Don't see the bubble?
Check that the snippet is on the published site (not just a draft), that you copied the whole
snippet, and that no other script is blocking it. See
[Troubleshooting](/saleszium/guide/troubleshooting).
:::
