This quickstart gets a useful chatbot live on your website fast. You'll create a knowledge base,
train the bot, customise it and install it - end to end.

:::tip Before you start
Have your website URL handy, and access to add a snippet to your site (or your developer on
standby). That's all you need.
:::

## 1. Add knowledge for the bot to learn from

Your chatbot answers using a **knowledge base**. Give it something to learn:

1. Open **Knowledge Base**.
2. Add a source - the quickest is to **point it at your website URL** so it reads your existing
   pages. You can also upload a document or write articles.

## 2. Train the chatbot

1. Select the sources you added.
2. Start **training**. Saleszium reads the content and builds the bot's understanding.
3. Wait for the "training complete" confirmation.

## 3. Customise its look and voice

1. Set the bot's **name**, **avatar** and **colours**.
2. Write a friendly **welcome message**.
3. Add a few **suggested questions** to guide visitors.

## 4. Install the widget

1. Copy your widget snippet from **Settings → Installation**.
2. Paste it into your website before `</body>` and publish.

Full details: [Install the widget](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/install-the-widget).

## 5. Test it live

1. Open your website and click the chat bubble.
2. Ask a question your content should answer.
3. Confirm the bot replies accurately.

:::success You're live
Visitors can now self-serve answers around the clock. When they need a person, the conversation
lands in your [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox).
:::
