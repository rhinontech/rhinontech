Your **workspace** is your organisation inside Saleszium. This is where you invite teammates,
control what they can access, and set the defaults everyone works from.

## Invite team members

1. Open **Settings → Team** (or the **Members** area).
2. Choose **Invite member** and enter their email address.
3. Pick a **role** for them (see below).
4. Send the invite - they'll receive an email to join your workspace.

The number of members you can add depends on your plan. See
[Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing) for the limits.

## Roles and access

Saleszium uses **role-based access** so people only see what's relevant to them. Typical roles:

- **Owner / Super admin** - full access to everything, including billing and settings.
- **Admin / Manager** - full access to day-to-day features, usually without billing controls.
- **Agent / Support** - focused on conversations, chats and tickets.

:::info Access follows the plan
Some areas unlock only on higher plans. If a teammate can't see a feature, check both their
**role** and your **plan**.
:::

## What to configure first

- Your **organisation profile** - name, logo and contact details.
- **Notification preferences** - so the team is alerted to new conversations and tickets.
- **Channels** - connect WhatsApp and email when you're ready (covered in their own guides).
