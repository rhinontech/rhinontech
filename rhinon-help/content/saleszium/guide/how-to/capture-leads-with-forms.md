Forms are the most reliable way to capture leads. This walkthrough builds one and connects it to
your CRM and an automation.

## Steps

1. Open **Forms** and create a new form.
2. Add the **fields** you need - keep it short (name and email go a long way).
3. **Embed** the form on your website (or include it in a campaign).
4. Submit a **test entry** and confirm a new lead appears in your
   [CRM](/saleszium/guide/features/crm).

## Make it do more

- Connect a [workflow](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation) so each submission is
  **assigned** and gets an **instant follow-up**.
- Add the new lead to a **segment** for future campaigns.

:::tip Speed beats everything
Leads go cold fast. Trigger an automated acknowledgement the moment someone submits, then have a
human follow up quickly.
:::
