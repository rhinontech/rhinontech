title: Connect WhatsApp
description: Link your WhatsApp Business number so customer messages arrive in your Saleszium inbox.

---

Connecting WhatsApp lets you handle one of your busiest channels from the same inbox as
everything else.

## Before you start

- Have access to an eligible **WhatsApp Business** number.
- Be an **Admin** (or higher) in your workspace.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Channels** and choose **WhatsApp**.
2. Follow the guided connection steps to link your **WhatsApp Business** account.
3. Authorise the connection when prompted.
4. Confirm the channel shows as **connected**.

## Verify it works

1. Send a message to your WhatsApp Business number from another phone.
2. Check that it appears in your [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox).
3. Reply from Saleszium and confirm the customer receives it.

:::info Follows WhatsApp's rules
Messaging is subject to WhatsApp's own policies (such as messaging windows and templates).
Availability depends on your business eligibility.
:::

:::tip Turn on notifications
Enable notifications so your team is alerted the moment a WhatsApp message comes in.
:::
