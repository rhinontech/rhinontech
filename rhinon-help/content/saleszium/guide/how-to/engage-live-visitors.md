Live visitor tracking is only valuable if you act on it. This walkthrough turns real-time
presence into conversations.

## Before you start

- The [widget](/saleszium/guide/getting-started/install-the-widget) must be installed.
- Open the [live visitors](/saleszium/guide/features/visitor-tracking) view.

## Steps

1. Watch the **live visitors** list to see who's online and what they're viewing.
2. Identify **high-intent** signals - time on the pricing page, a return visit, a demo page.
3. **Start a proactive chat** with a relevant, helpful opener.
4. If they engage, continue in the [inbox](/saleszium/guide/features/live-chat-inbox) and capture
   them as a [lead](/saleszium/guide/features/crm).

## Automate the timing

Instead of watching manually, use a [workflow](/saleszium/guide/features/workflow-automation) to
trigger a proactive message based on behaviour - for example, after a visitor spends a while on a
key page.

:::tip Lead with help, not a pitch
"Want a demo?" converts worse than "Happy to answer any questions about pricing." Offer value
first.
:::
