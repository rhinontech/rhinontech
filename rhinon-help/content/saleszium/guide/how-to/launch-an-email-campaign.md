This walkthrough sends an email campaign to a segment of your contacts and measures the response.

## Before you start

- Have a connected [email](/saleszium/guide/features/email) channel.
- Know the **audience** you want to reach (a list or segment in your CRM).

## Steps

1. Open **Campaigns** and create a new **email** campaign.
2. Choose your **audience** - a segment from your [CRM](/saleszium/guide/features/crm).
3. Write the **subject** and **body**. Start from a template and personalise it.
4. **Preview** the message and send yourself a test.
5. **Schedule** the campaign, or send it now.

## After it sends

1. Open the campaign's **performance** view.
2. Track **opens**, **clicks** and **responses**.
3. Note what worked, and apply it to the next send.

:::tip Segment for relevance
A smaller, well-targeted list usually beats a big generic blast. Use CRM segments to send the
right message to the right people.
:::

:::info Campaign limits
Your plan sets how many campaigns you can run - see
[Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).
:::
