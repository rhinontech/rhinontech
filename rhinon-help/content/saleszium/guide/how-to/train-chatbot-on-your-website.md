The fastest way to a useful chatbot is to train it on content you already have: your website.
This walkthrough takes you from zero to a trained bot.

## Before you start

- Know the **URL** of the site (or the specific sections) you want the bot to learn.
- Make sure those pages are **publicly reachable**.

## Steps

1. Open **Knowledge Base** and choose **Add source**.
2. Select the **website / URL** option and paste your address.
3. Let Saleszium **read the pages**. Larger sites take a little longer.
4. Review the imported content and remove anything you don't want the bot to use.
5. Start **training**.
6. Wait for the **training complete** confirmation.

## Test the result

1. Open the chatbot preview.
2. Ask questions your pages should answer (pricing, features, policies).
3. Note any weak answers.

## Improve it

- Add a [document](/saleszium/guide/features/knowledge-base) or write an article to fill gaps.
- Re-train after any change.

:::tip Re-train when content changes
Your website will keep evolving. Re-import and re-train whenever you publish important updates so
the bot never gives stale answers.
:::
