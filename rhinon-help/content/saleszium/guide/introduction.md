Saleszium is an **all-in-one customer engagement and sales platform**. It brings the tools a
growing business uses to talk to customers - an AI chatbot, live chat, WhatsApp, email, a CRM,
a sales pipeline, automations and campaigns - into a single workspace, so you don't have to
stitch together a dozen disconnected apps.

The idea is simple: **every conversation, lead and customer lives in one place**, and the
routine work of replying, qualifying and following up can be automated.

## Who it's for

- **Sales teams** managing leads, deals and a pipeline.
- **Support teams** handling chats, tickets and multi-channel messages.
- **Marketing teams** running email and chatbot campaigns and engaging website visitors.
- **Small and mid-sized businesses** that want one platform instead of five.

## What's inside

Saleszium is organised into a few connected areas:

- **AI Chatbot & Knowledge Base** - a chatbot you train on your own content to answer customer
  questions 24/7.
- **Conversations** - a unified inbox for live chat, WhatsApp and email.
- **CRM & Pipeline** - leads, contacts, companies, deals and forecasting.
- **Automation** - workflows that trigger actions automatically.
- **Marketing** - email, chatbot and LinkedIn campaigns, plus lead-capture forms.
- **Website tools** - live visitor tracking and SEO/performance audits.
- **Support** - tickets and a shared inbox.
- **Team & Billing** - members, roles, plans and usage.

## How the pieces fit together

A visitor lands on your website and the **chatbot** answers their questions using your
**knowledge base**. If they need a human, the conversation moves to your **inbox**. Their details
become a **lead** in the **CRM**, where they can be added to a **pipeline** and nurtured with
**campaigns** - and **automations** handle the repetitive steps in between.
