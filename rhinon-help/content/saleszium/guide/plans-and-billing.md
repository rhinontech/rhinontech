Saleszium is offered in tiers so you can start small and grow. Every plan includes the core
platform - chatbot, inbox, CRM and channels - with higher tiers raising your limits and unlocking
more capacity.

:::warning Confirm current pricing
The figures below reflect a recent snapshot and may change. Always confirm live pricing, limits
and inclusions on the official Saleszium pricing page or in your account before relying on them.
:::

## What's included on every plan

- Live website chat and the unified inbox
- AI chatbot with knowledge-base training
- CRM - leads, contacts, companies, deals and pipeline
- Email management and WhatsApp integration
- Direct calling and live visitor tracking

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly | Best for | AI tokens / mo | Team members | Training sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | ₹15,000 | Getting started | ~500K | Up to 5 | Up to 10 |
| **Growth** | ₹25,000 | Scaling teams | ~1M | Up to 15 | Up to 50 |
| **Scale** | ₹35,000 | High volume | ~2M | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing is offered at a reduced effective monthly rate. Higher tiers also raise limits on
**knowledge-base articles**, **SEO scans per week** and **active campaigns**.

## How usage works

- **AI tokens** are consumed as the chatbot reads your knowledge and writes replies. Each plan
  includes a monthly allowance.
- **Limits** (members, sources, articles, scans, campaigns) are set by your plan.
- When you approach a limit, Saleszium notifies you so you can upgrade if needed.

## Managing your subscription

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. View your current **plan and usage**.
3. **Upgrade or change** plans as your needs grow.
4. Access **invoices** and payment details.

:::tip Match the plan to your volume
The most common reasons to move up a tier are running out of AI tokens or needing more team
members. Watch those two first.
:::
