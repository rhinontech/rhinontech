Most issues fall into a few buckets. Work through the relevant section below.

## The chat widget doesn't appear

- Confirm the snippet is on your **published** site, not just a draft or preview.
- Make sure you pasted the **entire** snippet, before the closing `</body>` tag.
- Check that another script or a privacy/ad blocker isn't blocking it.
- Hard-refresh the page (clear cache) and try again.

## The chatbot gives weak or wrong answers

- The knowledge base may be missing the topic - add a
  [source](/saleszium/guide/features/knowledge-base) that covers it.
- You may have changed content without **re-training** - re-train after any update.
- The source may be thin or unclear - write a focused article for the question.

## A channel isn't receiving messages

- Re-check the connection in **Settings → Channels** - does it still show **connected**?
- For [WhatsApp](/saleszium/guide/how-to/connect-whatsapp), confirm your business number is
  eligible and the connection is authorised.
- For [email](/saleszium/guide/features/email), confirm the address is connected and routing in.

## I'm not getting notifications

- Check your **notification preferences** in settings.
- Confirm browser notifications are allowed for Saleszium.
- Make sure conversations are being **assigned** to you (or your team).

## I've hit a limit

- You may have reached a **plan limit** (tokens, members, sources, scans or campaigns).
- Review usage in **Settings → Billing** and consider upgrading - see
  [Plans & billing](/saleszium/guide/plans-and-billing).

:::info Still not resolved?
Gather what you tried and the exact behaviour you're seeing, then contact support so they can
investigate against your account.
:::
